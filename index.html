THREE SMALL STEPS

Understand difficult text without breaking your flow.

No copying into another app. No prompt writing. No account setup. Just select what is hard to read and ask Simplify 4 Me for help.

01 — HIGHLIGHT

Select the confusing part.

Highlight a sentence, paragraph, policy, article section, or other readable text on a normal webpage.

02 — SIMPLIFY

Choose what you need.

See simpler wording, extract key points, or identify hard words and their plain-English meaning.

03 — KEEP READING

Stay on the page.

The result appears beside your selection. Copy it, listen to it, safely replace a simple text selection, or close it and continue.
