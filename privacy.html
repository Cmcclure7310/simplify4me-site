Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Simplify 4 Me operates fully offline by default. Your replacement rules and settings are stored locally in your browser and are never transmitted to external servers.

When you enable optional online features such as dictionary or synonym lookup, requests may be sent to external dictionary APIs solely to retrieve definitions. No personal data is collected or stored by Simplify 4 Me.

We do not collect, share, or sell your data. For questions, contact hello@simplify4me.example.
