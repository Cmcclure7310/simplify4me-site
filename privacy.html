PRIVACY POLICY

Private by design.

This policy describes how the Simplify 4 Me Chrome extension handles information. Feature availability depends on your installed version.

The short version

Simplify 4 Me does not require an account and does not operate a cloud backend. The extension does not sell personal data, run behavioral advertising, or send your webpage text to Simplify 4 Me servers. Core reading analysis is performed by code packaged with the extension.

Website content

To provide its user-facing features, Simplify 4 Me can access readable text on webpages where the extension runs. This is necessary for features such as selected-text simplification, Key Points, Hard Words, Quick Read, Focus Mode, Complexity Lens, and optional site preferences.

That webpage text is processed locally by the extension for those features. Simplify 4 Me does not transmit the webpage text to a developer-operated server or third-party AI service.

Image text recognition

When you ask to read image text, the extension captures the visible content of your current Chrome tab and uses the selected crop for text recognition. The English recognition engine and language model ship with the extension. Capture and recognition take place on your device. The capture and editable transcript stay in memory until cleared or the review tab closes. If you pin a line, the extension saves the reviewed text and its source address in your local checklist.

Letter Decoder and calendar files

The Letter Decoder reads the documents you choose and finds dates, payment amounts, requirements and options on your device. Get ready to call can show phone numbers and account or case references found in those documents. When you choose Add to calendar, the extension creates a calendar file on your device and your browser saves it like any download. The file contains the date, the matching sentence from the page and the page address. Simplify 4 Me does not connect to or read your calendar.

My Words, Form Helper and suggestions

When you save a word to My Words, Simplify 4 Me stores the word, its meaning, the website name and the date in Chrome on your device. You can remove words or clear the list in Settings. Form Helper reads only the label of the form field you click into so it can explain it; it never reads, saves or sends what you type, and it ignores password fields. If you choose “Not on this site” for the decode suggestion, that website name is saved on your device.

Photos of letters and reply letters

Photos and scanned pages you open are read on your computer and are never uploaded or saved. Write back uses the name, phone number and address you enter to fill in a reply draft. By default these details stay in the current view. If you choose “Remember my name, phone and address on this computer”, Chrome stores them locally for future replies. Uncheck the option to delete the saved details. The extension does not send your contact details or drafts to the developer. You can choose to copy or print a draft yourself.

PDF reader, reminders and translation

When you open a PDF in Simplify 4 Me, it reads the file on your computer. For a PDF on a website, it downloads that file once from the same address Chrome used to show it; nothing is uploaded. If you turn on reminders, Chrome asks you to allow notifications, and reminders are created on your computer from the dates you tracked. Translation uses the translator built into Chrome on your computer when it is available. Call notes you type are never saved.

Paperwork inbox and usage count

If you choose Track these dates in the Letter Decoder, Simplify 4 Me saves the letter’s title, its web address, the dates it found and a short plain summary of each date in Chrome on your device, so it can show what is coming up and put a number on its icon. You can tick dates off or remove letters in Settings. Simplify 4 Me also keeps a simple count of how many times you have used it, on your device only, so it can ask once whether it is helping. None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Simplify 4 Me handles user data in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Read Aloud

When you choose Listen or Read Aloud, the extension passes the selected readable text to the speech-synthesis capability provided by Chrome and your operating system. Simplify 4 Me itself does not send that text to a developer-operated speech server. Some voices or operating-system speech services may be implemented by the browser or OS provider; their handling is governed by that provider's settings and privacy terms.

Settings stored on your device

Simplify 4 Me stores preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. These can include your simplification strength, Quick Read display preferences, custom replacement rules, speech speed, feature toggles, and the hostnames of websites you explicitly choose for “Always help on this site.”

These settings are used to provide and remember the extension experience. Simplify 4 Me does not include analytics or a developer-operated account-sync service.

When you pin a checklist item or save a reading spot, Chrome stores that item locally. Checklist items can include the quoted excerpt, its source address, your note, and whether the task is complete. Checkpoints keep the saved excerpt and reading view under a hash of the page address. You can remove checklist items and checkpoints within Quick Read. A passage saved for comparison lasts for the browser session. Terms, pinned definitions, and Keep exact choices last while the current reader stays open.

Permissions

The extension requests storage to remember your settings and saved reading data, contextMenus to offer reading actions from Chrome's right-click menu, and activeTab for temporary access to capture the active tab when you ask to read image text. The extension's content script runs on normal HTTP and HTTPS webpages because selection assistance, Focus Mode, Complexity Lens, and Quick Read must work on the pages you choose to read.

Simplify 4 Me does not run on Chrome internal pages such as chrome://, the Chrome Web Store's protected pages, or other browser-restricted locations where Chrome blocks extensions.

Data collection, sale, and sharing

  • No developer-operated user account.
  • No behavioral analytics.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No advertising profile.
  • No cloud AI or remote API used for simplification.
  • No remote JavaScript loaded by the extension.

Custom rules and exports

Custom rules you create remain in Chrome's local extension storage unless you export them. If you export settings, Chrome downloads a JSON file to a location you choose. If you import settings, the extension reads that file to restore supported preferences. Settings exports exclude checklist data and saved reading spots. Copy checklist places the quotes, source links, completion marks, and saved notes on your clipboard when you choose that action.

Changes to this policy

If a future version materially changes how information is processed, this policy will be updated before or alongside that release. A future feature that sends data to a server would require an updated disclosure and would not be silently added under this version's privacy promises.

Support

For help with Simplify 4 Me, visit the support page. You can also use the support contact shown on the Chrome Web Store listing.

Simplify 4 Me is a reading aid, not a substitute for the original source. For legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, or other high-stakes material, review the original wording and consult an appropriate qualified source when needed.
