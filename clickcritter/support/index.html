Support

Make ClickCritter work for you.

Help for the 2.0 customization update. Store availability may still show the preceding release while submission/review is pending.

Get started

Pin ClickCritter in Chrome, open its popup, choose a theme or favorite, then open Theme Library for the full collection. The master switch instantly restores the system cursor.

Simple & Advanced

Simple Mode focuses on complete packs. Enable Advanced mode for detailed sliders, Page Accents, per-site rules and local-time schedules. Preview a card before applying it.

Create a pet/photo cursor

In My Themes choose Pet Photo Cursor. PNG/JPG/WebP images up to 5 MB and 8192 pixels per side are processed locally. Crop/zoom, choose a mask, place the hotspot, capture and name your theme. This is manual local masking, not automatic AI background removal.

Save, share and import themes

Save a customized style to My Themes. Edit, duplicate, favorite, delete or export it. Theme files can contain your cropped photo: only share files with people you intend to receive those images. Import validates supported local theme data.

Random & daily rotation

Choose all themes, favorites or a collection as the source. Favorites may include custom themes. Empty favorites do not select from another source. Rotation uses local calendar dates and works while Chrome runs.

Local schedules & site rules

In Advanced Settings create local-time ranges and user-selected domain profiles. Longest matching domain wins; schedules use the first matching range. Manual theme changes pause automatic changes for an hour. Alarms may be delayed and are not exact real-time scheduling.

Presentation shortcuts

Alt+Shift+C toggles the master switch. Alt+Shift+P toggles Presentation Mode. Assign or change commands at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. Choose large cursors, black/white contrast, yellow halo, optional spotlight/crosshair and click ripple.

Effects or cursor not visible

Refresh pages after an update. Chrome internal pages, Chrome Web Store, built-in PDFs and protected surfaces keep the system cursor. Some cross-origin frames, closed shadow-root widgets and fullscreen overlays may retain page cursors. Check site rules, master switch and reduced-motion preferences.

V1 progress

V1 settings, XP, level math and 15 achievements are retained locally with a raw migration backup. Fun Stats is optional. Existing users keep stats enabled; fresh installs start with tracking off. Uninstalling removes local storage.

Performance & privacy

Effects have strict limits and never intercept clicks. Disable ambient/trails or use Clean & Quiet for the simplest experience. No browsing-history logs, typed text, passwords or cookies are collected. No extension data is uploaded.

For feedback or a reproducible issue, use the support controls on the existing Chrome Web Store listing. Include Chrome version, selected theme and what you expected. Avoid posting private photos or sensitive page data.
